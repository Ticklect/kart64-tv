A redesigned Kart64 TV preview: stronger racing theme, clearer menus and less scrolling.

- New launcher and pause-menu layouts, with a prominent Resume action.
- Live controller diagrams: button highlights and moving stick indicators.
- Side-by-side Xbox/PS5-layout controller cards.
- Shorter binding groups for buttons, D-pad/clicks and analog sticks.
- Redesigned volume and render-scale settings, plus reliable controller focus.

Includes the previous preview's saved remapping, disconnect pause, actual boot stages and protected Save & Exit. PC real-game integration checks and automated tests pass. **Physical controllers, real-TV performance and multiplayer remain unverified.** [Controls and test evidence](https://github.com/Ticklect/kart64-tv/blob/v1.0.2-preview2/docs/TV-CONTROLS.md).
