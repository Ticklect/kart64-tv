Original-game menus for Kart64 TV.

- Original MK64 title artwork, background, menu headings and sprite font.
- Compact pause menu over your race, with matching controller, sound and loading screens.
- Matching startup screen for Samsung/LG packages and the Android launcher.
- Menu/diagnostic hints stay out of the HUD during controller play.

**The artwork and font come from your own game files.** Nothing is bundled in the public downloads. Existing users: prepare and transfer your game once with this Windows version to get the native skin; an APK-only update keeps readable fallback text. Game saves are preserved.

123 automated checks and PC game/launcher integration checks pass. Android TV emulator rendering is checked; physical controllers, real-TV performance and multiplayer still need hardware validation.
